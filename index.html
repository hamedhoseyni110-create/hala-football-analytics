    Prediction

    How the prediction is calculated (no black box)

    1. Take each team's last 10 league matches and count wins, draws and losses → each team's win/draw/loss rates. A team's attacking record pushes its own win chance up; a team's loss rate pushes the opponent's win chance up.
    2. Take their head-to-head meetings this season and count wins for Team A, draws, wins for Team B → head-to-head rates.
    3. Blend them: home score = 0.35 × (A win rate) + 0.35 × (B loss rate) + 0.30 × (A head-to-head win rate), and the same pattern for draw and away win. If the teams never met, the head-to-head part is dropped and the two teams' form count 50/50.
    4. Normalize the three scores so they add up to 100%. Percentages are rounded.
    InputTeam ADrawTeam B

    Weights: each team's form 35% + head-to-head 30% (or 50/50 form when no meetings). This is a simple descriptive model, not a betting signal — it only looks at results, not injuries, lineups or xG.
